07 FAQ

Before you ask.

The questions new students and clients send me most — anything else, just message me.

How do the classes run?

Live on Google Meet with screen sharing — you see my screen, I see yours. We pick a time that fits you (evenings and weekends, EAT), and after every session you get notes and practice exercises.

What do I need to start?

Just a phone, tablet or laptop and internet. For computer packages I'll walk you through installing everything you need in the very first class — no experience required.

What do classes cost?

Rates depend on the package and how often we meet — message me on WhatsApp for the current price list and course bundles. The 20-minute intro call is always free.

Can I learn with a friend or colleague?

Yes — groups of up to 5 at a discounted rate per person. Same syllabus, same personal notes for everyone.

Which languages do you teach in?

English or Kiswahili — whichever you're more comfortable with. Class notes are in English.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes — a certificate of completion when you finish a full course track, plus a portfolio project for the web and coding classes.

How do we start?

Book the free intro on WhatsApp. We talk about your goals, agree on a schedule, and start the same week.
